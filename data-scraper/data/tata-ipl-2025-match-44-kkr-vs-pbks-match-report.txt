Inclement weather and uninterrupted rain at the Eden Gardens in Kolkata led to the abandonment of Match 44 of the TATA Indian Premier League (IPL) 2025 between Kolkata Knight Riders (KKR) and Punjab Kings (PBKS). As a result of the washout, both teams were awarded one point each. 
A post shared by IPL (@iplt20)
This result means the Punjab Kings move to fourth place in the points table with 11 points from nine games, while the Knight Riders occupy seventh place with seven points from nine games. Gujarat Titans, Delhi Capitals, and Royal Challengers Bengaluru, all with 12 points, hold the first three places respectively. 
The game began with Priyansh Arya and Prabhsimran Singh stitching a strong partnership of 120 runs, before captain Shreayas Iyer chipped in some runs in a partnership with Prabhsimran, helping the Kings post a total of 201/4 in 20 overs. Prabhsimran top scored with 83 while Priyansh scored 69. Vaibhav Arora topped the charts for the bowling side, picking 2 wickets for 34 runs from his quota of four overs. 
The chase began with KKR scoring seven of their first over, before the covers came on, and persistent rain and lightning led to the game being called off.
